The playback voice

The sight-reading section can play a passage back so a student hears it before trying it. A synthesised tone would sound like a keyboard, so the app plays a real violin instead — yours. This page records it: about seventy takes, one note each, and it hands them back as one zip file.

  • Room: dry and quiet. No reverb pedal, no hall — the app adds its own.
  • Microphone: your RODE, about 30–40 cm from the instrument, slightly off the f-hole rather than over it.
  • Level: play your loudest note first and set the gain so the meter peaks in the green. Never touch it again.
  • Bow: same speed and contact point all the way through, so the takes match each other.
  • Keys: space starts and stops a take, enter keeps it, r redoes it, h hears the pitch.

Nothing is uploaded. The takes stay in this tab until you download them.
